XP-GSI - data sources and attribution
=====================================

The tables in the data/ folder are derived from the open data sources below.
Each file states its source and license in its "source" / "license" fields.
Data under different licenses is kept in separate files; the plugin combines
them only at runtime.

Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1.0/
  The following files are Derivative Databases under the ODbL 1.0 and are
  made available under the same license:

  data/airports/*.json       Parking statistics per airport (airlines and
                             aircraft types parked per stand area), derived
                             from adsb.lol globe_history data
                             (https://adsb.lol, https://github.com/adsblol).
                             The "terminals" and "osm_stands" fields contain
                             terminal outlines and gate positions from
                             OpenStreetMap.
  data/airline_fleets.json   Airline fleets and brand splits, derived from
                             adsb.lol globe_history data. Fleet counts are
                             blended with the number of aircraft per type
                             listed in airline articles on Wikipedia (facts).
  data/terminal_assignments.json
                             Terminal zones from OpenStreetMap.

  Contains information from adsb.lol, made available under the ODbL 1.0.
  Any rights in individual contents of these databases are licensed under
  the Database Contents License, https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1.0/
  Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA) 4.0
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
  The following files are adaptations of text from English Wikipedia
  (https://en.wikipedia.org), by Wikipedia contributors, and are made
  available under CC BY-SA 4.0:

  data/wiki_airlines.json    Airline shares per airport, computed from the
                             "Airlines and destinations" section of each
                             airport's article (the article is named in the
                             file's "article" field per airport).
  data/terminal_airlines.json
                             Airlines per terminal, from the same lists,
                             split by terminal by hand-written rules.

CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)
  Wikidata (https://www.wikidata.org): airline ICAO codes, countries and hubs
  used to build the files above.

Public domain
  OurAirports (https://ourairports.com/data/): airport types in
  data/airports_eligible.json.

Facts
  data/major_hubs.json lists airports by annual passenger numbers (ACI World
  and FAA figures as reported on Wikipedia). Only airport codes are used.

Our own data
  data/csl_livery_overrides.json, data/regional_brands.json,
  data/related_types.json, data/retired_types.json,
  data/stand_overrides.json, data/cdu_adapters.json: hand-written by the
  XP-GSI authors. No CSL model, texture or text is included in XP-GSI.

Airport stand positions are never shipped: XP-GSI reads them from the
scenery installed in your X-Plane.
